# Shopify AI Design Prompt — TapFive Review

Build a premium e-commerce store for "TapFive Review" (tapfivereview.com) selling an NFC Google review stand (Model 215) — a countertop display that lets customers tap their phone or scan a QR code to leave a Google review in 8 seconds. Target buyers: small local businesses (salons, cafés, dental offices, auto shops, hotels).

## Brand & Style
- Luxury "black and gold" aesthetic — like a high-end jewelry or premium tech brand
- Background: deep obsidian black #0A0B0E, alternating with slightly lighter charcoal sections (#0B0C10, #12141C)
- Accent: metallic gold gradient — champagne #F3E5AB → gold #D4AF37 → deep bronze #996515
- Primary text: off-white #F8F9FA; secondary text: muted slate #94A3B8
- Subtle grain/noise texture over backgrounds; soft gold radial glow behind the hero
- Cards: dark charcoal with thin gold borders at low opacity, large 24px rounded corners, deep soft shadows, gentle lift on hover
- Section dividers: thin gold gradient lines fading out at the edges

## Typography
- Headings: elegant high-contrast serif (Playfair Display), bold, tight letter spacing, 48–60px on desktop
- Body: clean modern sans-serif (Satoshi, or Inter/Manrope as fallback)
- Eyebrow labels above every heading: small uppercase gold text with wide letter spacing
- Key phrases inside headings highlighted with gold gradient text

## Buttons
- Primary CTA: pill-shaped, metallic gold gradient fill, dark text, soft gold glow shadow, lifts slightly on hover (label: "Get Your Stand Now")
- Secondary: pill-shaped, transparent with thin gold border and champagne text
